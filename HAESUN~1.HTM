Haesun Moon

Toronto, Canada

Executive coach and communication scientist; founder, Canadian Centre for Brief Coaching

Communication scientist and executive coach based in Toronto, founder of the Canadian Centre for Brief Coaching and originator of the Dialogic Orientation Quadrant. Her research examines how coaching conversations actually work at the level of language.

Why this placement

The Dialogic Orientation Quadrant is original, published work with an empirical basis — a model of what happens inside a coaching conversation, derived from analysing conversations rather than asserted from experience. That is the engagement-with-evidence marker, and very little coaching writing meets it.

Shortlisted for the Thinkers50 Coaching & Mentoring Award in 2023 and listed on Coaches50. Three books, one confirmed with Page Two.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • No website is linked. Her centre's site is a JavaScript application that serves no content to crawlers and her personal domain is empty — a real problem for a reader trying to reach her, and the reason she ranks ninth rather than higher.
  • Two of her three books have no verifiable publisher.
  • Reported as shortlisted, not a recipient.
