Ranked #3 in the Q3 2026 edition of Top 10 Female Executive Coaches. Assessed against the published methodology.

Sally Helgesen

Chatham, New York, USA

Executive coach, author and speaker on women's leadership

Executive coach, speaker and author whose work on women's leadership spans four decades. Inducted into the Thinkers50 Hall of Fame in 2022 and awarded the Institute for Management Studies Lifetime Achievement Award in 2023.

Why this placement

Eight books across Hachette and Berrett-Koehler is the largest body of published work on this list, and it is sustained rather than clustered — a four-decade record of returning to the same problem and revising the answer, which is exactly the development-over-time marker the methodology looks for.

The IMS Lifetime Achievement Award in 2023 is independent of the Thinkers50 and Goldsmith network entirely, which under the interlink adjustment makes it worth more than a second honour from inside that circle. Her Hall of Fame induction in 2022 is confirmed on Thinkers50's own site.

Editorial notes and exclusions

  • Her location is Chatham, New York. The widely-repeated Chatham, Massachusetts version originates in an error on her 100 Coaches profile.
  • A "#1 in leadership by Global Gurus" claim is dated: she was #1 in 2024, #3 in 2025, and absent from the 2026 list. Global Gurus is in any case a partly public-vote listing, not an award, and is not counted as recognition here.
